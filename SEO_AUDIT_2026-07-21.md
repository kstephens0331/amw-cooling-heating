# AMW Cooling & Heating — SEO Gap Audit

**Date:** 2026-07-21
**Method:** 5 parallel agent audits (technical, on-page content, GSC coverage/indexing, off-page/competitive via Ahrefs, local SEO/GBP) synthesized into one prioritized list. 33 raw findings, deduped to 23. 137 tool calls, 6 agents.

---

## Executive Summary

The July 7–8 overhaul added 71 pages, but every audit lens converges on one dominant problem: the 39 templated service×town pages (and several new location pages) are functionally invisible to Google, and the templated/duplicate content across them is the most likely cause of both that invisibility and active cannibalization between the location hub, service hub, and homepage for the same queries. Nearly all real site traffic still flows through two pre-overhaul pages (homepage + one blog post), while two audit areas — Ahrefs backlink/keyword data and Google Business Profile — could not be accessed at all, leaving off-page authority and local-pack visibility completely unverified. Baseline technical hygiene (sitemap, canonical tags, core NAP consistency) is solid, so the highest-leverage work is de-templatizing the new pages and restoring the two blocked data sources, not infrastructure repair.
